Project 2 · Lifecycle marketing

Romeo Waiting

Colour-led womenswear, made in small runs in Melbourne.

Objective

Give the brand a complete email system in its own voice, the “love letters from the Waiting Room”. Turn sign-ups into first orders, recover abandoned carts and bring lapsed customers back.

Goal

Make every stage of the customer journey earn its place: a first order, a second piece, and a clean list. Cart, care and winback emails each have a clear job.

What we designed

5 flows · 2 newsletters · 17 emails. Designed in Figma and implemented in Klaviyo.

  • 3Welcome series
  • 2Browse abandonment
  • 3Abandoned cart
  • 4Post-purchase
  • 3Winback
  • 2Newsletters

Four of the emails

Each email has one job. Discount codes and personal details are hidden.

Planned measures once live: first-order conversion, cart recovery, repeat purchase and winback rate, against the brand's own baseline.
