Project 5 · Campaign copy and design system

Emily Watson

Swimwear and demi-couture, made in Brunswick, Naarm (Melbourne).

Objective

Write and design the label's November to December campaign calendar: subject line, preview text, heading, body and button for 25 sends, on an email design system built from the storefront.

Goal

Sell through the season in the label's own voice. Subject lines lead with the product, prices are exact, and sales show only the Sale badge and a struck-through price, with no pressure lines.

What we designed

25 sends · 6 templates. Designed in Figma and implemented in Klaviyo.

  • 25Campaign sends with full copy
  • 6Lifecycle templates
  • 53Products catalogued
  • 1Voice and design system

Four of the emails

Each email has one job. Discount codes and personal details are hidden.

Planned measures once live: revenue per send, click rate, sale sell-through and unsubscribe rate, against the brand's own baseline.
