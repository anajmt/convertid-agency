Project 6 · Campaign emails

With Harper Lu

Print-led womenswear and swim, Australia.

Objective

Design and write five campaign emails on one With Harper Lu design system: cross-promotion, VIP early access for Black Friday, FAQ, follow on social and loyalty.

Goal

Lift order value and get ready for Black Friday. Pairings raise basket size, the VIP list builds demand before the sale, the FAQ removes doubts before checkout, and loyalty brings buyers back.

What we designed

5 campaign emails. Designed in Figma and implemented in Klaviyo.

  • Cross-sell
  • VIP early access
  • FAQ
  • Follow on social
  • Loyalty

Four of the emails

Each email has one job. Discount codes and personal details are hidden.

Planned measures once live: average order value, VIP sign-ups, click-to-order rate and repeat purchase, against the brand's own baseline.
