Project 1 · Lifecycle marketing

Madre Natura

Handcrafted in Sydney from natural fibres, in small runs, with lifetime mending.

Objective

Turn newsletter sign-ups and hesitant shoppers into buyers while protecting full price. The only discount is the 10% welcome offer. Everything else sells through story, materials and mending.

Goal

Build trust before the sell, answer the price question with value, and bring buyers back for a second piece. Each email has one job and one button.

What we designed

4 flows · 1 launch campaign · 16 emails. Designed in Figma and implemented in Klaviyo.

  • 4Welcome series
  • 3Abandoned cart
  • 2Browse abandonment
  • 4Post-purchase
  • 3Launch campaign

Four of the emails

Each email has one job. Discount codes and personal details are hidden.

Planned measures once live: first-order conversion, cart recovery and revenue per email, against the brand's own baseline.
