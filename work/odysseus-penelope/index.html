Project 3 · Newsletter catalogue

Odysseus & Penelope

Bamboo and resort wear, designed in Melbourne.

Objective

Plan and design the brand's Black Friday to Christmas newsletters: 14 sends from 12 Nov to 29 Dec, every Tuesday and Thursday, built on three reusable templates.

Goal

Drive sales and checkout with one main button per email, steady offer and gift messages, and founder stories that build the brand between sales.

What we designed

14 sends · 3 templates. Designed in Figma and implemented in Klaviyo.

  • 7Sale and drop emails
  • 2Founder stories
  • 5Holiday and checkout emails

Four of the emails

Each email has one job. Discount codes and personal details are hidden.

Planned measures once live: revenue per send, click-to-order rate and sale-period revenue, against the brand's own baseline.
