How we grow email revenue

Six service areas, each tied to a business problem and a measure

A

Welcome flows

Problem: New subscribers join the list but don't make their first purchase.

Actions

  • Map a 3-email welcome journey.
  • Introduce the brand and its product value.
  • Feature bestsellers and customer reviews.
  • Present a first-purchase incentive only when commercially appropriate.
  • Test timing, subject lines, and calls to action.

Measure

  • First-purchase conversion rate
  • Welcome flow revenue
  • Revenue per recipient
  • New subscriber-to-customer conversion

B

Abandoned cart & checkout recovery

Problem: Shoppers show purchase intent but leave before completing an order.

Actions

  • Build a 2–3 email recovery sequence.
  • Address shipping, sizing, returns, and product questions.
  • Remind customers about the items they considered.
  • Test incentives only when necessary.

Measure

  • Cart recovery rate
  • Recovered orders
  • Recovered revenue
  • Revenue per recipient

C

Post-purchase & repeat purchase

Problem: The customer relationship ends after the first transaction.

Actions

  • Create thank-you and order education emails.
  • Share styling, care, and product recommendations.
  • Introduce complementary products.
  • Build a second-purchase journey based on buying behaviour.

Measure

  • Repeat purchase rate
  • Time to second purchase
  • Revenue from post-purchase flows
  • Customer lifetime value, where reliable data is available

D

Browse abandonment & winback

Problem: Interested visitors and inactive customers are not being re-engaged.

Actions

  • Create browse abandonment journeys.
  • Segment inactive subscribers and past customers.
  • Send relevant new arrivals and product recommendations.
  • Use a sunset process for persistently inactive subscribers.

Measure

  • Re-engagement rate
  • Winback conversion
  • Recovered revenue
  • Unsubscribe and spam complaint rates

E

Campaign strategy

Problem: Brands send inconsistent promotional emails without a clear commercial objective.

Actions

  • Build a monthly campaign calendar.
  • Plan new arrivals, product launches, seasonal moments, promotions, and editorial content.
  • Give each campaign one primary CTA.
  • Balance commercial messages with brand storytelling.
  • Review campaign performance after each send.

Measure

  • Campaign-attributed revenue
  • Revenue per recipient
  • Click-to-order rate
  • Conversion rate
  • Unsubscribe rate

F

Segmentation & optimisation

Problem: Every subscriber receives the same message regardless of their behaviour.

Actions

  • Segment new subscribers, first-time buyers, repeat buyers, VIP customers, and inactive subscribers.
  • Test subject lines, creative, content, timing, and offers.
  • Review deliverability and list health.
  • Optimise based on revenue and conversion data.

Measure

  • Segment-level revenue
  • Revenue per recipient
  • Conversion rate
  • List growth and engagement
  • Deliverability indicators

Access to your email platform and ecommerce store, or screenshots of your current flows and campaigns, is enough for the free initial review. The full audit needs read access to your reporting.

Do you guarantee revenue?

No. Results depend on traffic, product demand, pricing, customer behaviour, deliverability, and your starting performance. We set out the assumptions and report against them each month.

We use the same attribution source and reporting window when comparing periods. Platforms attribute revenue differently, so we agree the method with you before the first report.

Why is there a three-month minimum?

Flows and segments need time to collect data, and tests need enough sends to read. Three months is the minimum period in which the work can be properly built, tested, and reviewed.
