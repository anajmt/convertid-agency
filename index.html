FAQ

Questions we hear

Do you replace our team?

No. We work on our own or alongside your in-house person, taking strategy, flows and reporting off their plate.

Which platforms do you use?

Klaviyo, on stores built with Shopify or WooCommerce. If you use another platform, the audit shows what moving would involve.

Why a 3-month minimum?

Flows take about six weeks to show results and a calendar needs a full cycle.

What do you need from us?

Access to your email platform and store, your brand assets, and about 3 hours in the first two weeks. After that, a short weekly approval.

How do we approve emails?

You see every design and every send before it goes out. Nothing is sent without your sign-off.

Who owns the work?

You do. Designs, copy, flows and data stay in your accounts and files, even if you leave.
